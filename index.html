My darling,

Every year this day comes around, and every year I find myself thinking the same thing how did I get so lucky. Today isn't just your birthday. It's the day the most beautiful, most important person in my life came into this world, and I don't take that for granted for a single second.

You are not just my girlfriend. You are my best friend, my peace, my favourite conversation, the person I want to tell everything to first. Every small thing about you your laugh, the way you talk with your hands, your little habits I've memorised without even trying makes my life better just by being in it.

I wish more than anything that I could be with you today. Holding your hand, watching you open your eyes this morning, seeing that smile in person instead of in a photo. The distance between us right now genuinely aches. I miss our conversations that run too long, I miss the quiet moments where nothing needs to be said, I miss simply having you close. No matter how many miles sit between us, you are always the thing my mind wanders back to.

You are so pretty it still catches me off guard sometimes, and so cute in a hundred small ways you probably don't even notice about yourself. But what I love most isn't how you look, it's your heart. The way you care for the people around you. The way you make ordinary days feel like they matter.

On your birthday, my only wish is that you feel as loved as you make everyone around you feel. I hope your day is soft and joyful and exactly as beautiful as you are. Thank you for choosing me, for staying close even from far away, and for every memory we've built together so far I want a lifetime more of them with you.

Happy birthday, my love. I love you more than I know how to put into words, and I can't wait to be back in your arms.

— always yours
